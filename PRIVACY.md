# HearHere privacy policy

_Last updated: 9 October 2026_

HearHere is a Chrome extension that reads web page text aloud. This policy explains what data it handles. The short version: **the developer of HearHere collects nothing.** There are no accounts, no analytics, no tracking and no HearHere servers.

## What HearHere handles, and where it goes

**Text you choose to hear.** When you select text, rest your cursor on a paragraph in Hover Read mode, or use a HearHere shortcut, the extension reads that text from the page.
- With the **system voice** (the default), the text is spoken by your computer's built-in voices and never leaves your device.
- With **OpenAI** or **ElevenLabs** selected, the text is sent directly from your browser to that provider's API to generate the audio, using your own API key. HearHere sends only the text you asked it to read. It does not send page URLs, browsing history or anything else.

**Your API keys.** If you add an OpenAI or ElevenLabs API key, it is stored only in your browser on this computer (`chrome.storage.local`). It is not synced and is sent only to the provider it belongs to, as part of requests you trigger.

**Settings and usage counts.** Your preferences (speed, voice, limits) are stored with Chrome's storage. A character count of what you've generated with paid voices is kept locally so HearHere can show usage and enforce your daily limit.

**Cached audio.** Audio generated by OpenAI or ElevenLabs is cached on your computer (IndexedDB) so that replaying the same text is free. You can clear it anytime from HearHere's settings. Uninstalling the extension removes it.

## What HearHere does not do

- It does not collect, sell or share personal data with the developer or with any third party other than the voice provider you choose.
- It does not track your browsing or read pages you don't ask it to read.
- It does not use your data for advertising, credit or any purpose unrelated to reading text aloud.

## Third-party providers

When you choose a premium voice, the text you send is handled under that provider's own terms and privacy policy:
- OpenAI: https://openai.com/policies/privacy-policy
- ElevenLabs: https://elevenlabs.io/privacy-policy
